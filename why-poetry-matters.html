Why learning something new matters

Learning helps us understand the world and make better decisions.

A poem does not always need complicated language. Sometimes a simple image, a familiar memory or a carefully chosen word can make a reader pause and think.

Culture & literature has a rich tradition of expressing love, loss, hope, longing and the everyday details of life. When reading a verse, take a moment to notice its meaning, mood and imagery rather than rushing to the next line.
