How to research a topic before sharing it

Give a poem time: notice its images, tone and the feeling behind each line.

Start with a clear question. Look for trustworthy sources, check dates and compare claims. Keep notes, separate facts from opinions and link to useful references so readers can verify important details.

Try writing your own short explanation in plain language. This helps readers connect with the poem without replacing the original work.
